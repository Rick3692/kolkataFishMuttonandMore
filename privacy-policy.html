Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Kolkata Fish, Mutton & More ("we", "the app") is a shopping app for ordering fish, mutton, chicken, grocery, vegetables, sweets, puja items and achar & hajmi, based in Hafeezpet, Hyderabad 500049.

What information the app stores

When you place an order or save your details, the app stores your name, WhatsApp/phone number, delivery address and cart contents on your own device (in the app's local storage) so you don't have to retype them next time. This information is not uploaded to any server owned by us.

How orders are sent

When you tap "Send order on WhatsApp", the app opens WhatsApp with your order details pre-filled, addressed to our store number. The message is only sent when you press Send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to messages sent through WhatsApp. We use your name, number and address only to confirm and deliver your order.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your personal information with advertisers or third parties. The app does not use advertising, tracking, analytics, or location services, and does not collect payment card details.

Deleting your data

You can remove your saved details at any time using "Log out" on the Login page, or by clearing the app's data in your phone's settings. To ask us to delete order messages held in our WhatsApp, contact us using the details below.

Children

The app is a general shopping app and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
